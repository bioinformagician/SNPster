Model selection. The report first removes validation rows that do not contain a positive share of the user's target ancestry. A model must also have at least 70% matched variants to become the primary/top model. Each trait card then selects the primary eligible model by: highest AUROC, then highest target-ancestry share in the evaluation sample, then largest evaluation sample size. Additional retained models for the same trait are kept and shown in the expandable model-details section, including models below the primary-model variant-match threshold. Risk categories are based on the selected best model percentile for traits with fewer than 4 retained scores, and on the median retained-model percentile when more than 4 scores are available. For traits with more than 4 retained scores, the main percentile bar shows the observed retained-model percentile range, mean, and translucent IQR band. The 95% CONFIDENCE INTERVAL is shown only as a separate sub-card.
How to read the odds and hazard fields. The odds and hazard cards show individualized percent changes calculated from the user z-score. The caption under each value shows the published source effect size, displayed as OR per SD or HR per SD. If no odds or hazard effect is available for a trait, the card displays NA.

Methodological notes

Important limitations. Polygenic scores estimate inherited genetic predisposition; they are not diagnostic tests. AUROC, odds ratio, and hazard ratio values come from specific validation cohorts and may not transfer perfectly to every ancestry, age group, sex, genotyping array, or imputation pipeline. The individualized risk-effect calculation assumes that the source odds ratio or hazard ratio is reported per standard deviation of the PRS. If the original publication used a different effect unit or comparison group, the transformed percentage may be misleading and should be flagged in your score-selection database.
