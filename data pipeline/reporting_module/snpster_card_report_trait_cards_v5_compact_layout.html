Model selection. The report first removes validation rows that do not contain a positive share of the user's target ancestry. The target ancestry is currently hardcoded as European. Each trait card then selects the primary model by: highest target-ancestry share in the evaluation sample, then highest AUROC, then largest evaluation sample size. Additional models for the same trait are kept and shown in the expandable model-details section. Risk categories are based on the selected best model percentile: <5 Very low, 5-19 Lowered, 20-79 Normal, 80-94 Increased, and ≥95 Highly Increased.
How to read the risk effect fields. The odds and hazard fields are not the raw published OR/HR per standard deviation. They are individualized percent changes calculated as ((OR_or_HR ** user_z_score) - 1) * 100. This assumes the source OR/HR is reported per 1 standard deviation increase in PRS. If a source publication used another unit, that metric should be interpreted cautiously.

Methodological notes

Important limitations. Polygenic scores estimate inherited genetic predisposition; they are not diagnostic tests. AUROC, odds ratio, and hazard ratio values come from specific validation cohorts and may not transfer perfectly to every ancestry, age group, sex, genotyping array, or imputation pipeline. The individualized risk-effect calculation assumes that the source odds ratio or hazard ratio is reported per standard deviation of the PRS. If the original publication used a different effect unit or comparison group, the transformed percentage may be misleading and should be flagged in your score-selection database.
