How it works

From a raw genotype file to an interpretable result.

The pipeline standardizes, imputes, filters and scores your genome before turning the output into a report designed for comparison rather than certainty theatre.

01

Upload

Start with a supported raw genotype export from your testing provider.

02

Harmonize + impute

Variants are standardized to a common build and missing genotypes are statistically inferred.

03

Quality control

Low-confidence and poorly supported variants are filtered before score calculation.

04

Interpret

Compare percentiles, agreement, evidence quality, ancestry context and model performance.
